With the Privacy Friendly Battleship app, you can play the popular game “Battleship” conveniently on your smartphone. The aim of the game is to sink all of your opponent's ships by taking turns “shooting” at your opponent's playing field.

The game offers two game modes:
- Player vs. computer
- Player vs. player

Players can choose between 5x5 and 10x10 playing fields and place their ships freely. The number of ships can be selected before the game. Ships of sizes 2 (submarines), 3 (destroyers), 4 (cruisers), and 5 (battleships) are available.

What distinguishes Privacy Friendly Battleship from other similar apps?

1. No permissions
Privacy Friendly Battleship does not use any permissions.
By comparison, the top ten similar apps available in the Google Play Store require an average of 6.1 permissions (as of November 2017). These include access to networks, the internet, or location data.

2. No annoying ads
Many other free apps in the Google Play Store display annoying ads, which can shorten battery life and consume data volume, among other things.

The app belongs to the group of privacy-friendly apps developed by the SECUSO research group at the Technical University of Darmstadt. More information at: https://secuso.org/pfa
